# Privacy Policy for Expense & Budget Tracker

**Effective Date:** November 3, 2026

**Expense & Budget Tracker** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our Android mobile application.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Storage

- **100% Local Device Storage:** All financial data, expenses, notes, categories, and transaction dates entered into Expense & Budget Tracker are stored **strictly offline on your local device** using an Android Room Database.
- **No Third-Party Transmission:** We do **NOT** collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal financial data, identification, or device information on external servers or with third parties.

---

## 2. Permissions & Data Access

- **No Unnecessary Permissions:** The application does not request sensitive device permissions (such as contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone).
- **Offline First:** The core features of the application function completely offline without requiring an active internet connection.

---

## 3. Data Control & Retention

- You maintain total control over your data.
- All stored expenses can be deleted individually within the app or entirely by clearing the app data in Android system settings or uninstalling the application.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Expense & Budget Tracker does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
